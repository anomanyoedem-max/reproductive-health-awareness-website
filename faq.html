Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about PCOS / PMOS, endometriosis, symptoms, and seeking care.

The supplied write-up describes PMOS, previously known as PCOS, as a condition involving hormonal, metabolic, and reproductive functions that may affect menstrual health, fertility, skin, hair growth, and other aspects of health.

Endometriosis is a chronic condition in which tissue similar to the lining of the uterus is found outside the uterus. It can cause inflammation, pain, and adhesions.

No. PCOS / PMOS is primarily associated with hormonal, metabolic, and reproductive changes. Endometriosis involves endometrial-like tissue outside the uterus. Some symptoms may overlap.

Both may be associated with difficulty becoming pregnant for some people. Neither condition automatically means pregnancy is impossible. Discuss individual concerns with a qualified healthcare professional.
