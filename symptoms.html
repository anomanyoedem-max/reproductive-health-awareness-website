Know the signs

Symptoms

Symptoms vary in type and severity. Some overlap between conditions, and other health issues may cause similar symptoms.

PCOS / PMOS symptoms

Symptoms may relate to menstrual, hormonal, skin, hair, fertility, and metabolic changes.

Irregular periods

Periods may be irregular or absent, often associated with changes in ovulation.

Hair-growth changes

Some people notice increased facial or body hair, or thinning hair on the scalp.

Skin changes

Acne or oily skin may be associated with hormonal changes.

Fertility concerns

Changes in ovulation may make becoming pregnant more difficult for some people.

Endometriosis symptoms

Pain and recurring cyclical symptoms are important features to recognize.

Painful periods

Menstrual pain may be severe enough to interrupt daily activities.

Pelvic pain

Pain can also occur outside menstruation or follow a recurring cycle.

Pain with urination or bowel movements

These symptoms may become more noticeable at particular points in the cycle.

Fatigue or fertility concerns

Fatigue and difficulty becoming pregnant can occur, although experiences vary.

Important: Symptoms alone do not establish a diagnosis. Seek professional assessment when symptoms are severe, persistent, recurrent, or interfere with daily activities.
